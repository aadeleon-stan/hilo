Let's add another mode to this game. This will be the "Daily" mode, which is a daily challenge that is the same all day (reset at midnight Pacific Time) for all players (this is only relevant once the app is hosted live on the web).

## Single round
The daily challenge is a single round of the hilo game.

## Winnable
The board state and target score must be winnable with skilled play.

## Static
The game must be the same for all players during the day; the initial board and target score are selected randomly (conditioned on being winnable) at the start of the day and then static throughout the day.

## Stats
We can show players an "Optimal" and "Par" energy bank based on a solver of the daily game. Or, instead of building a solver, we can just track a leaderboard to show what the best player has done so far and the distribution of players' scores (with a mean or median score) so far.

## Sharability
Give the players a nice modal with an easy image to share about how they performed. Celebrate if they made par; celebrate even more if they are close to optimal.
