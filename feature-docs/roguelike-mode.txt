# Upgrades, Items, and Relics
The output from the actual planning task is captured in roguelike-mvp-set.md. Here are some notes to consider in relation to that file.

## Upgrades
Your suggested list in roguelike-mvp-set.md is way too small. A player is bound to see every possible upgrade since they are offered three upgrades nine times. Some easy ways to help:
1. As originally suggested, all decade upgrades should be limited to one pool (player can choose which to apply to). So getting a "Unlock 10s decade" upgrade can be beneficial twice.
2. As originally suggested, the ones-digit removals should come in pairs and be tied to a specific pool. Then "Remove 5 from Pool A and 9 from Pool B" is a different upgrade than "Remove 9 from Pool A and 5 from Pool B."
3. The "+X to Y" upgrades can be broken into more incremental pieces, e.g., instead of a single "+40 max energy" you can offer "+10 max energy" multiple times (or "+1 to optimal bonus cap" or "+10% round-end refund").
4. "Guarantee a [decade] value in each pool" should only apply to one pool (similar to point 1) and should be available multiple times for multiple decades. So it shouldn't just be for 10-19 but probably also 20-29 and maybe even 30-39, and we should allow the player to get up to, say, 4 fixed in the same pool.

A few of the upgrades you've been testing don't really make sense:
- "Pool A limited to 10-49" is a silly upgrade for MVP -- just force the player to do it themselves by collecting the correct decades. There may be room for it as a stronger upgrade variant later on, but for the initial playtest version of the game it feels arbitrary and redundant.
- "+1 number per pool" requires new UI. Forget it.
- "+1 reroll of the board's largest number" limits skill expression. The player should be free to reroll any number.
- "Keep smallest number into the next round" seems silly because if you get an 11 you can just keep rolling it over. Forget it.

## Items
Regarding items, we also have way too few. In three shops with three offers each, we will repeat items very quickly, so suggest some new items.

Also, your issue with the "Revive a factor" item seems like nonsense in light of the suggestion to let the player choose and remove a different (live) number from the same pool, which would keep the turn count stable.

Finally, why does the "shopping app" issue matter? We're not balancing for it, we're just building a feature to playtest.

## Relics
I don't see how the digit-swap relic or inventory slots depend on economy. And again, we're not balancing, it's just a feature to playtest.
